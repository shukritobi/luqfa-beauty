# LUQFA editorial design system

**Concept:** Skincare as editorial object, not another beauty storefront template.

- **Main action:** discover a product and proceed to a trusted shopping experience.
- **Audience:** Malaysian skincare shoppers following LUQFA or its cosmetic-chemist founder.
- **Real material:** original LUQFA product photography, founder-history photos, verified product names, variants and price snapshots.
- **Signature:** large restrained display typography adjacent to LUQFA's own collection photograph.
- **Mood:** tactile paper, pale blush, ink-brown, deep mulberry, natural editorial whitespace.
- **Type:** Playfair Display (expressive publication headlines) with DM Sans (quiet utilitarian UI).
- **Palette:** paper #fbf7f2, blush #f1dce0, mulberry #542f3d, ink #37272a, stone #efe6df.
- **Composition:** off-centre hero, open cardless product photography, double-page founder story, linear routine finder, oversized footer masthead.
- **Restraints:** no gradients, invented social proof, unsubstantiated claims, meaningless animation, or dead marketing forms.
- **Responsive:** desktop split page, accessible mobile navigation, two-column mobile product shelf, reduced-motion fallback.

**Design responsibility:** Images and commerce remain subject to brand approval, accessibility checks and real-device verification before launch.
